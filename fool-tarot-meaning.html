The Fool Card Overview

The Fool is numbered 0 in the Major Arcana — the number of infinite potential. Depicted as a carefree traveler on the edge of a cliff, The Fool represents new beginnings, innocence, and faith in the journey ahead. When this card appears it often signals a fresh start, a leap of faith, or an invitation to trust the unknown.

Key symbols

  • White rose — purity and innocence
  • Cliff — risk and adventure
  • Dog — loyalty and protection
  • Sun — optimism and guidance

Upright Meaning of The Fool

Upright, The Fool invites you to embrace the unknown with an open heart. It's about curiosity, freedom, and the courage to begin.

General Upright Meaning

New journeys, opportunities, optimism, and a willingness to act without overthinking.

The Fool in Love

Upright The Fool often points to a new relationship or a refreshing phase. Expect lightness, playfulness, and emotional openness.

The Fool in Career

Indicates new projects, job changes, or creative ventures. A good omen for following your passion, provided you balance inspiration with practical steps.

Reversed Meaning of The Fool

Reversed, The Fool warns about poor planning, naivety, or fear of change. It asks you to slow down and check details.

General Reversed Meaning

Recklessness, hesitation, or acting without foresight.

The Fool Reversed in Love

May signal jumping into relationships too quickly, emotional immaturity, or unclear intentions.

The Fool Reversed in Career

Watch for impulsive career moves, ignoring red flags, or missing important practicalities.

Yes or No — The Fool

Upright: Generally Yes — go ahead if guided by intuition and preparation.
Reversed: Not yet — reconsider and plan before leaping.
